## Why this candidate exists

v11 (r2) wedges under long-context load: the scheduler refuses admission with
`reason=capacity` while `running=0` and KV usage is 0%, `/health` keeps
answering 200, and nothing is scheduled again. Reproduced on this box on
2026-09-06 three ways — reserve 16 (~30 s), reserve 0 (~4.5 min), and a single
89%-KV chat. Root cause and fixes (upstream numbering):

- **0004** the align-mode admission cap billed null slots as real demand
  (~10× inflation for long prompts), gated by the default-on
  `scheduler_reserve_full_isl`, so it bit even with the reserve off.
- **0005** a request blocked solely by the reserve now ages out after
  `VLLM_MAMBA_STATE_PROTECT_AGE` attempts.
- **0006** deferred frees drain on 0-token steps too (inert here — no KV
  transfer connector).

r2.1 also **removes r2's "Fix B" 512-token decode-aware prefill chunk cap**
upstream ("obsolete after the cache-wipe fix; interval-only decode protection
verified at ITL p95 22 ms"). Decode protection is now `--prefill-schedule-interval 8`
alone. The ITL cells below are the re-qualification of that change.

## Admission deadlock — the r2 failure, re-tested on v11.1

The 2026-09-06 reproducer that wedged v11 in 30 s, re-run on v11.1 with the
reserve **on** at 16: a concurrent burst plus a whale at the head of the queue.

| | v11 (r2) | v11.1 (r2.1) |
|---|---|---|
| Concurrent mixed burst | queued forever | 41 rounds, 328/328 requests OK |
| Whale (272K) mid-burst | never scheduled | 200 OK, 86 s |
| Preemptions / aborts / errors | — (wedged) | 0 / 0 / 0 |
| Steady state after load | `run=0 wait=N kv=0%`, stats stopped | `run=0 wait=0 kv=0%`, stats ticking, smoke 84 ms |

No reserve-aging-escape warning fired, so **fix 0004 (real-footprint billing)
alone kept admission flowing** — the escape (0005) was never needed. That is the
direct confirmation of the diagnosis: the inflated billing was the load-bearing
bug; the reserve only amplified it.

### r2.1-specific: shared-prefix churn under a whale

- 15/15 turns exact, whale 200 OK in 92.7 s, **0 preemptions**
- per-turn prefix cache hit fraction: min 0.846, mean 0.847 (the shared prefix

### Concurrency — the Fix-B-removal check

From `probe-inference-throughput.sh`:

| Round | Decode ITL p95 | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| c1-short | 23 ms | single stream |
| c8-short (8 concurrent) | 50 ms | decode stays bounded at full seq concurrency |
| c4-long (4× ~23K prefill) | 37–437 ms | tail is prefill-interleave, not steady decode |

Steady-state decode ITL p95 holds at **~22–23 ms** at C1 and long context, and
**50 ms under 8-way concurrency** — matching upstream's stated 22 ms basis for
removing the chunk cap. The c4-long p95 spikes to 437 ms on the two requests
whose decode overlapped a fresh 23K prefill chunk; the two that hit warm cache
(17,920 / 8,960 cached tokens) held 37 ms. Steady decode is unaffected; the
tail is the expected cost of interleaving a long prefill without the chunk cap.

MTP mean acceptance length across rounds: 2.80 (c1) → 2.88 (c8) → 3.52 (c4-long).

## Qualification decision

**Benchmark-ready, and the r2 admission deadlock is fixed.** Every v10 gate
passes on v11.1, the r2 wedge no longer reproduces under its own trigger with
the reserve on, and decode latency under concurrency re-qualifies the removed
chunk cap. Not auto-promoted to production: the candidate is retained at
`restart=no` pending any separate release-policy gate, and the near-max
retrieval cell should be re-run at the exact 358,900 token point with a
tokenizer-accurate prompt sizer before a production sign-off if that exact
figure is required.
