## Gate verdicts — all 6 FAILs decompose into two benign causes

1. **No-think preamble + echo quirk (5 FAILs: text, retrieval ×3,
   mixed-traffic shorts, prefix-churn exacts).** With `thinking: false` the
   model emits a preamble sentence and then repeats the answer
   (`"The code is QX-DZ7AGXRK.QX-DZ7AGXRK"`), breaking every exact-match
   assertion. Manual verification: the embedded codes were **found correctly
   at 128K and 358.9K**. Under production thinking mode the preamble lives in
   `reasoning_content`, so content is clean.
   ⚠️ Standing caveat: do not wire strict extraction against this checkpoint
   with thinking disabled.
2. **c1 assertion assumes exhaustion (1 FAIL).** The gate requires three
   800-token `finish="length"` runs; the model answered well in ~500 tokens
   and stopped early. Concise behaviour, wrong assumption — the decode numbers
   in the FAIL record are the valid measurement.
